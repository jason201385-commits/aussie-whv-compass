## If you need help now

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#urgent-title)

| Situation | First action in Australia |
| --- | --- |
| Life-threatening illness or injury, serious accident, violence, fire or crime in progress | **Call 000** and ask for ambulance, police or fire. Give your location and stay on the line. Emergency services can arrange a free interpreter after connecting you. |
| Not sure how urgent a health problem is | Call **healthdirect 1800 022 222** for 24/7 nurse advice, or use its service finder. Mobile call charges may apply. |
| Immediate suicide risk or a severe mental-health crisis | Call 000. For crisis support, call **Lifeline 13 11 14**, text **0477 13 11 14** or use Lifeline chat. |
| Domestic, family or sexual violence, including workplace sexual harassment | Call 000 if danger is immediate. Otherwise call **1800RESPECT 1800 737 732**, text **0458 737 732**, or use online chat or video call. |
| Poisoning or suspected overdose, but the person has not collapsed | Call the Poisons Information Centre on **13 11 26**. If the person has collapsed, stopped breathing or is in immediate danger, call 000. |
| You need an interpreter for a non-emergency service | Call **TIS National 131 450**, name your language and ask to be connected to the organisation. |

Call 000 [Find a health service](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services) [How to call 000](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/calling-triple-zero)

## 1. Check Medicare eligibility from your passport, not your visa nickname

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#medicare)

**Most temporary visitors do not have Medicare.** Australia has Reciprocal Health Care Agreements with 11 countries, but eligibility, enrolment, the length of cover and the services included depend on the particular agreement and your visitor status.

Do not assume that every Working Holiday Maker is uninsured, or that an RHCA gives full Australian-resident cover. Check the Services Australia page for the passport you hold. New Zealand and Ireland have different visitor arrangements from countries whose eligible visitors enrol and receive a reciprocal Medicare card.

- Open the official RHCA visitor list and choose your country.

- Check whether you must enrol after arrival and what evidence is required.

- Read the limits: public-patient care, medicines, dates and exclusions can differ.

- Keep travel or visitor cover for gaps such as ambulance, private care, repatriation, dental or services outside the agreement.

[Check RHCA eligibility](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/reciprocal-health-care-agreements) [Medicare enrolment](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare)

Sources: [Services Australia — Reciprocal Health Care Agreements](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/reciprocal-health-care-agreements) · [enrolling in Medicare](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare) | checked 2026-08-30

## 2. Buy a promise you have checked, not a policy name

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#cover)

Overseas Visitors Health Cover and travel insurance are not interchangeable labels, and products under the same label can have different benefits. This site does not rank insurers and has no affiliate relationship with them.

| Ask in writing before buying | Why it matters on a working holiday |
| --- | --- |
| Are GP, specialist, pathology and imaging visits covered? | Some visitor policies focus on hospital treatment and leave routine or outpatient care partly or fully unpaid. |
| Which public and private hospital costs are covered? | A hospital may charge an overseas visitor directly. Insurer benefits may not equal the provider's fee. |
| Does ambulance cover apply in every state and territory? | Medicare does not cover ambulance services and state arrangements differ. |
| Are farm, construction, hospitality, driving or other paid manual-work injuries excluded? | A general travel policy may restrict paid work, particular occupations or hazardous activities. |
| What waiting periods, pre-existing-condition rules and permanent exclusions apply? | A policy can be active while a particular condition or service is still excluded. |
| Are mental-health care, pregnancy, dental, optical, physiotherapy and prescription medicines covered? | Hospital and extras benefits differ, and pharmaceutical limits can leave large gaps. |
| What excess, co-payment, annual limit and benefit percentage will I pay? | "Covered" does not necessarily mean zero out-of-pocket cost. |
| Is emergency evacuation or repatriation included? | Returning home for treatment is not the same benefit as Australian hospital care. |
| Must I call the insurer before non-emergency treatment? | Pre-authorisation, provider networks, direct billing and claim documents differ. |
| What happens if payment fails or my visa, address or job changes? | A lapsed policy or unreported eligibility change can affect a later claim. |

**Useful written question:** “I will hold a Working Holiday Maker visa and may do paid farm, warehouse, hospitality or construction work in more than one state. Please identify the exact clauses for work injuries, ambulance, hospital, outpatient care, pre-existing conditions, waiting periods, medicines, excesses and repatriation.” Save the reply and the policy wording that applied on the purchase date.

Source: [Australian Government Private Health Insurance Ombudsman — Overseas Visitors Health Cover](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/overseas/overseas_visitors_health_cover.htm) | checked 2026-08-30

## 3. Choose the right level of care

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#care)

### Pharmacist

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#main-content)

Medicine questions, minor symptoms and advice on whether another service is needed. A pharmacist cannot replace emergency assessment or a GP when symptoms are serious or persistent.

### GP or after-hours service

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#main-content)

General illness, prescriptions, referrals, follow-up and non-emergency mental-health concerns. Ask the clinic whether it accepts overseas visitors and what the consultation may cost.

### Urgent care

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#main-content)

For urgent but not immediately life-threatening problems. "Medicare Urgent Care Clinic" funding does not prove that a person without Medicare will receive free care; confirm eligibility and fees before relying on it.

### Emergency department or 000

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#main-content)

For serious illness or injury such as chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, severe burns or major accidents. Do not delay emergency care to check a bill.

- If unsure, call healthdirect on 1800 022 222. A nurse can help decide what type of care to seek.

- Use the service finder for a nearby GP, pharmacy, after-hours service, urgent care clinic or hospital.

- For non-emergency care, ask the provider and insurer about likely fees, direct billing, pre-authorisation and documents.

- Keep itemised invoices, receipts, referral letters, test results and claim reference numbers.

Sources: [healthdirect — emergency departments](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/hospital-emergency-departments) · [after-hours health services](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/after-hours-health-services) · [paying for healthcare](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/how-do-i-pay-for-healthcare) | checked 2026-08-30

## 4. Make your medicines and health record portable

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#medicines)

- Carry a plain-language list of conditions, allergies, medicines, active ingredients, dose and prescriber contact. Brand names can differ in Australia.

- Keep prescription medicines in original labelled packaging and carry the prescription or a clinician's letter. Check special rules before travel; some products require a permit or cannot be brought in.

- The traveller's exemption commonly limits eligible medicines to a three-month supply and to personal use or an immediate family member travelling with you. It does not make every substance legal.

- Declare biological, herbal or traditional products when border rules require it. Do not give, sell or share imported prescription medicine.

- Store insurance details and an emergency contact somewhere available even if your phone is lost or locked.

[TGA medicine rules](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/consumer-information-and-resources/travelling-medicines-and-medical-devices/entering-australia)

## 5. Treat a work injury as both a health event and a record

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#work-injury)

- **Get safe and get care.** Call 000 for a serious injury. Do not keep working to protect a roster, accommodation or specified-work plan.

- **Tell the employer as soon as safely possible.** Ask how the incident is being recorded and which insurer or workers' compensation authority applies.

- **Keep independent evidence.** Record the time, place, task, hazard, witnesses, injury, first aid, medical advice, roster and messages. Keep copies outside an employer-controlled account.

- **Ask the correct state or territory authority.** Workers' compensation and work health and safety are administered by jurisdictions, not by Safe Work Australia itself.

- **Do not accept visa threats as medical advice.** Visa holders and migrant workers have workplace protections. An employer cannot cancel a visa; only Home Affairs can grant, refuse or cancel one.

[Find your safety or compensation authority](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/whs-regulators-and-workers-compensation-authorities-contact-information) [Fair Work migrant-worker help](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants)

## 6. Mental-health support is a route, not a test you must pass

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#mental)

Isolation, unstable work, language pressure, unsafe housing and money stress can become overwhelming. This page does not diagnose a condition. You do not need to wait for a crisis or know the perfect words before contacting a GP, counsellor or support service.

| Need | Contact | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life is in danger or immediate suicide risk | **000** | Emergency response. Stay with the person when safe and follow the operator's instructions. |
| Crisis support | Lifeline **13 11 14**, text **0477 13 11 14**, or 24/7 chat | Crisis support is delivered in English. See Lifeline's language-access page or use TIS for phone interpreting options. |
| Anxiety, depression or a difficult day | Beyond Blue **1300 22 4636** or 24/7 webchat | Free counselling for people in Australia; local call costs may apply. |
| Ongoing assessment or treatment | A GP or mental-health professional | Ask about fees, insurance benefits and telehealth eligibility before non-emergency care. |

[Lifeline](https://www.lifeline.org.au/) [Beyond Blue](https://www.beyondblue.org.au/get-support/talk-to-a-counsellor)

## 7. Violence and sexual harassment are safety issues, not job conditions

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#violence)

**Immediate danger:** call 000. If someone monitors your phone or bill, use a safer device when possible. 1800RESPECT warns that its call or text numbers may appear on an itemised phone bill.

- 1800RESPECT supports anyone in Australia affected by domestic, family or sexual violence, including workplace sexual harassment. Phone, text, online chat and video-call services are available 24/7.

- Phone interpretation can be requested. Text support is currently English only; use TIS National for a telephone interpreter.

- At work, sexual harassment is unlawful. Protection applies in connection with work, including to workers, future workers and some self-employed people.

- Prioritise safety before confronting a person, collecting evidence or returning to collect belongings. A support service can help make a safety plan.

[Open 1800RESPECT](https://www.1800respect.org.au/) [Workplace sexual-harassment help](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/workplace-sexual-harassment)

Sources: [1800RESPECT — access and language support](https://www.1800respect.org.au/accessibility) · [contact and phone-bill safety](https://1800respect.org.au/FAQ) · [Fair Work — sexual-harassment rules](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/workplace-sexual-harassment/rules-about-workplace-sexual-harassment) | checked 2026-08-30

## 8. Add distance, heat and transport to the health plan

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/health/#remote)

- **Before a remote shift:** know the exact worksite, emergency procedure, first-aid contact, communication coverage, drinking-water access, heat controls, chemical hazards and required PPE.

- **Before driving:** tell someone the route and arrival time, check fuel and water, avoid driving fatigued, and keep location information available offline.

- **Before accepting employer-linked transport or housing:** keep enough money and an independent contact route to leave unsafe work or accommodation.

- **Before relying on your phone:** save the worksite address or coordinates, insurance number, emergency contact and medicine list offline. The Emergency+ location tools are described on healthdirect's 000 page.

Sources: [Safe Work Australia — working in heat](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/working-heat) · [healthdirect — location and Emergency+](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/calling-triple-zero) | checked 2026-08-30

- Medicines and doses
