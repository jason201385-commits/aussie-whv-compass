## If you need a workable money plan today

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#quick-plan)

- **Protect the exit first.** Keep return-travel money and an emergency buffer separate from normal spending.

- **Use verified pay, not a job advertisement.** Check the Award, classification, employment type and penalties in Fair Work P.A.C.T.

- **Budget for every week.** Work may stop, but accommodation, food, phone and transport do not. The planner below models 46 income weeks and 52 expense weeks.

- **Choose housing and transport together.** A cheaper room can cost more if every shift needs a car or an expensive late-night trip.

- **Delay a car purchase until the full cost is visible.** Price registration, insurance, fuel, servicing, tyres, repairs and resale risk before paying a deposit.

- **No food or safe place tonight?** Use [Ask Izzy](https://askizzy.org.au/) to search nearby support. For immediate danger call 000.

### Weekly survival

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Record accommodation, food, transport, phone, health, job equipment and debt commitments. Use a weekly figure because rent and many wages are discussed weekly.

### Exit and shocks

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Keep a separate amount for leaving unsafe work or housing, urgent travel, a medical gap, replacing a phone, moving or one major vehicle repair.

**AUD 5,000 is a visa-evidence reference, not a personal budget guarantee.** Home Affairs says evidence of enough money is usually AUD 5,000 plus enough to leave Australia for a first subclass 462 visa. Requirements can differ by application and change over time; check the current first-visa page for your subclass before applying.

Official sources: [Home Affairs — first subclass 462 visa](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-462/first-work-holiday-462) · [first subclass 417 visa](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/first-working-holiday-417) | checked 2026-08-30

## 2. Know what a pay number does and does not mean

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#pay-tax)

| 2026–27 reference | Amount or rate | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| National Minimum Wage | **AUD 26.44 per hour** or AUD 1,004.90 for 38 hours | Adult award-free reference. Most employees are covered by an Award or agreement. |
| Award-free casual National Minimum Wage | **AUD 33.05 per hour** | Includes 25% casual loading. Do not use it as the legal rate for every job. |
| Standard WHM tax schedule | 15% to 45,000; 30% to 135,000; 37% to 190,000; 45% above | Progressive annual estimate for WHM income. Treaty, residency, deductions and other income may change an assessment. |
| No TFN withholding | 45% | The 2026 WHM withholding schedule says 45% if a TFN is not provided and no permitted exception applies. |
| Super guarantee reference | 12% of ordinary time earnings when eligible | Not simply 12% of every payment. Overtime and eligibility can change the base. |

- Use the [Fair Work Pay and Conditions Tool](https://calculate.fairwork.gov.au/) with the actual industry, role, age, level, employment type, day and time. An advertisement or this planner is not a pay check.

- Match each payslip against your own start, finish and break records. Check gross pay, rate, hours, penalties, deductions, tax, net pay and super information.

- A registered employer can withhold 15% up to AUD 45,000 of payments to a WHM with a TFN; the applicable rate changes above that threshold. Withholding during the year and the final tax assessment are not always identical.

- Super is not spendable weekly cash. A temporary resident may later be eligible for a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment, with separate tax rules.

Official sources: [Fair Work — 1 July 2026 minimum wage](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/newsroom/media-releases/2026-media-releases/july-2026/20260701-minimum-wage-increase-media-release) · [Taxation Administration (Withholding Schedules) Instrument 2026, Schedule 15](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L00716/latest/text) · [ATO — super guarantee](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee) | checked 2026-08-30

## 5. Compare food by unit price, then reuse ingredients

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#food)

No supermarket is always cheapest. Compare the price per kilogram, litre or 100 grams for the product you will actually use. A special label, large pack or loyalty offer is not proof of lower unit cost.

[**Find an ALDI store**Compare the actual unit price and travel cost](https://www.aldi.com.au/storelocator) [**Check Coles specials**Set the store before relying on price or stock](https://www.coles.com.au/on-special) [**Check Woolworths specials**Recheck the final price and unit price](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/browse/specials) [**Learn unit pricing**Official ACCC comparison guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/unit-prices-for-groceries)

**A low-waste base basket:** oats, rice or pasta; eggs, canned beans or lentils; canned tomatoes; frozen vegetables; onions; and one flexible protein. Plan three meals that use the same ingredients before opening the specials page.

### Overnight oats

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Mix oats, milk or a suitable alternative and fruit; refrigerate. Add yoghurt or seeds only if they fit your budget and dietary needs.

### Egg, rice and frozen vegetables

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Heat cooked rice and vegetables thoroughly, then add egg. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate.

### Lentil tomato pasta

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Simmer canned tomatoes, drained lentils and onion; combine with pasta. Freeze an extra portion if it will not be eaten soon.

### Chicken and vegetable tray bake

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#main-content)

Cut pieces evenly and cook the centre of poultry to at least 75°C. Keep raw and ready-to-eat utensils separate.

## 6. Buy ordinary clothing cheaply, but treat PPE as safety equipment

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#clothes)

[**Salvos Stores**Search nearby second-hand stores](https://www.salvosstores.com.au/stores) [**Vinnies shops**Search local second-hand stores](https://www.vinnies.org.au/shops) [**Red Cross shops**Find a second-hand shop](https://shop.redcross.org.au/store-locator) [**Compare Kmart basics**Check current price, fit and durability](https://www.kmart.com.au/) [**Compare BIG W basics**No price or quality guarantee from this site](https://www.bigw.com.au/)

- For ordinary clothing, inspect seams, zips, stains, smell, fabric wear and washing instructions. Compare the total cost of travel and replacement, not only the tag price.

- For safety boots, high-visibility clothing, gloves, masks, hearing or eye protection, ask what the actual task and workplace risk assessment require.

- Safe Work Australia says a business must manage PPE duties, including selecting suitable PPE, fit, maintenance and information or training. A worker should not guess a substitute from a fashion shelf.

## 7. Buy or sell a used car without confusing a platform with protection

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#car)

These direct commercial links are unpaid routes, not endorsements. Listings, ranking, identity checks, fees and dispute support differ. Aussie WHV Compass does not accept vehicle listings, deposits, identity documents or payments, and does not act as seller, buyer, agent, mechanic, insurer or dispute handler.

[**Search used cars on Carsales**Independent commercial marketplace](https://www.carsales.com.au/cars/used/) [**Search Gumtree vehicles**Verify the seller and vehicle independently](https://www.gumtree.com.au/s-cars-vans-utes/c18320) [**Search Facebook Marketplace**A profile or group history is not proof of ownership](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/category/vehicles) [**List through Carsales**Read current fees and safety rules first](https://www.carsales.com.au/sell-my-car/) [**List through Gumtree**Keep payment and handover evidence](https://www.gumtree.com.au/cars/sell-my-car) [**List on Facebook Marketplace**Never rely on a payment screenshot](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/create/vehicle)

- **Get the VIN before money moves.** Match it on the vehicle, registration record and seller's evidence.

- **Run the official PPSR search on the day of purchase or the day before.** The online vehicle search costs AUD 2 and can identify a registered security interest, stolen status or written-off status.

- **Know what PPSR does not prove.** It does not tell you the amount owing, current owner, complete history, odometer accuracy, fines or mechanical condition.

- **Use an independent qualified mechanic.** A roadworthy or safety certificate is not a full pre-purchase inspection and local requirements differ.

- **Check current registration and seller authority through the state or territory service.** Compare name, VIN, registration and transfer documents. Do not copy another jurisdiction's deadline or form.

- **Price insurance before purchase.** Do not assume registration includes cover for damage to your own car or another person's property.

- **Use a written receipt and cleared payment.** Record the parties, date, price, VIN, registration, odometer and included items. Do not release a car or keys because a buyer shows a transfer screenshot.

**Consumer protection depends on who sold the car.** ACCC says consumer guarantees apply to cars bought from a licensed dealer. A private sale does not provide the same protections, although title, undisturbed possession and no hidden debts or charges still matter. State and territory vehicle rules also apply.

Official sources: [PPSR — used vehicle search](https://www.ppsr.gov.au/carcheck) · [Moneysmart — buying and running a car](https://moneysmart.gov.au/student-life-and-money/buying-and-running-a-car) · [ACCC — new and second-hand cars](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/specific-products-and-activities/new-and-second-hand-cars) · [Scamwatch — buying and selling scams](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au/types-of-scams/buying-and-selling-scams) | checked 2026-08-30

## 9. When the plan no longer covers essentials

[Section permalink](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/cost/#short)

- Stop optional transfers, subscriptions and purchases. Protect food, safe housing, essential medicine, phone access and transport to verified work or support.

- Contact the provider before missing a payment. Ask about a hardship team or payment arrangement; do not borrow from an unsolicited message or social-media lender.

- Use [Moneysmart's financial counselling route](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managing-debt/financial-counselling) for free, confidential help and confirm service scope.

- Search [Ask Izzy](https://askizzy.org.au/) for nearby food, housing, health and other support.

- If work is unpaid or deductions are unexplained, preserve rosters, messages, payslips, bank records and your hours, then use the [English workplace help route](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/work/#problems).

- If a scam caused the loss, call the bank through an independently verified channel and follow the [English recovery sequence](https://www.aussiewhvcompass.com/lang/en/scam/#already-happened).
