Cost of living and money plan

Turn wages and weekly costs into a plan you can challenge before the money runs out. Sources checked 2026-08

Editorial status: this is a complete English editorial draft, not an Australian Government publication and not yet reviewed by a native-speaking Australian tax, financial-counselling or consumer-services professional. It is general planning information, not tax, financial, legal, dietary or vehicle advice. Current official pages and your own records always control.

If you need a workable money plan today

  1. Protect the exit first. Keep return-travel money and an emergency buffer separate from normal spending.
  2. Use verified pay, not a job advertisement. Check the Award, classification, employment type and penalties in Fair Work P.A.C.T.
  3. Budget for every week. Work may stop, but accommodation, food, phone and transport do not. The planner below models 46 income weeks and 52 expense weeks.
  4. Choose housing and transport together. A cheaper room can cost more if every shift needs a car or an expensive late-night trip.
  5. Delay a car purchase until the full cost is visible. Price registration, insurance, fuel, servicing, tyres, repairs and resale risk before paying a deposit.
  6. No food or safe place tonight? Use Ask Izzy to search nearby support. For immediate danger call 000.

1. Build a money map before choosing a city

Arrival money

Visa evidence, flights, insurance, temporary accommodation, bond, basic setup and the gap before the first cleared pay are different costs. Do not spend the same dollar twice.

Weekly survival

Record accommodation, food, transport, phone, health, job equipment and debt commitments. Use a weekly figure because rent and many wages are discussed weekly.

Exit and shocks

Keep a separate amount for leaving unsafe work or housing, urgent travel, a medical gap, replacing a phone, moving or one major vehicle repair.

AUD 5,000 is a visa-evidence reference, not a personal budget guarantee. Home Affairs says evidence of enough money is usually AUD 5,000 plus enough to leave Australia for a first subclass 462 visa. Requirements can differ by application and change over time; check the current first-visa page for your subclass before applying.

Official sources: Home Affairs — first subclass 462 visa · first subclass 417 visa | checked 2026-08-30

  • Use the Fair Work Pay and Conditions Tool with the actual industry, role, age, level, employment type, day and time. An advertisement or this planner is not a pay check.
  • Match each payslip against your own start, finish and break records. Check gross pay, rate, hours, penalties, deductions, tax, net pay and super information.
  • A registered employer can withhold 15% up to AUD 45,000 of payments to a WHM with a TFN; the applicable rate changes above that threshold. Withholding during the year and the final tax assessment are not always identical.

5. Compare food by unit price, then reuse ingredients

No supermarket is always cheapest. Compare the price per kilogram, litre or 100 grams for the product you will actually use. A special label, large pack or loyalty offer is not proof of lower unit cost.

AFind an ALDI storeCompare the actual unit price and travel cost CCheck Coles specialsSet the store before relying on price or stock WCheck Woolworths specialsRecheck the final price and unit price ULearn unit pricingOfficial ACCC comparison guidance
A low-waste base basket: oats, rice or pasta; eggs, canned beans or lentils; canned tomatoes; frozen vegetables; onions; and one flexible protein. Plan three meals that use the same ingredients before opening the specials page.

Overnight oats

Mix oats, milk or a suitable alternative and fruit; refrigerate. Add yoghurt or seeds only if they fit your budget and dietary needs.

Egg, rice and frozen vegetables

Heat cooked rice and vegetables thoroughly, then add egg. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate.

Lentil tomato pasta

Simmer canned tomatoes, drained lentils and onion; combine with pasta. Freeze an extra portion if it will not be eaten soon.

Chicken and vegetable tray bake

Cut pieces evenly and cook the centre of poultry to at least 75°C. Keep raw and ready-to-eat utensils separate.

These flexible meal ideas are not nutrition, allergy or medical advice. Adapt ingredients for allergies, health, culture and religion. Sources: ACCC — unit prices for groceries · Food Standards Australia New Zealand — food safety basics | checked 2026-08-30

  • For safety boots, high-visibility clothing, gloves, masks, hearing or eye protection, ask what the actual task and workplace risk assessment require.

7. Buy or sell a used car without confusing a platform with protection

These direct commercial links are unpaid routes, not endorsements. Listings, ranking, identity checks, fees and dispute support differ. Aussie WHV Compass does not accept vehicle listings, deposits, identity documents or payments, and does not act as seller, buyer, agent, mechanic, insurer or dispute handler.

CSearch used cars on CarsalesIndependent commercial marketplace GSearch Gumtree vehiclesVerify the seller and vehicle independently FSearch Facebook MarketplaceA profile or group history is not proof of ownership CList through CarsalesRead current fees and safety rules first GList through GumtreeKeep payment and handover evidence FList on Facebook MarketplaceNever rely on a payment screenshot
  1. Get the VIN before money moves. Match it on the vehicle, registration record and seller's evidence.
  2. Run the official PPSR search on the day of purchase or the day before. The online vehicle search costs AUD 2 and can identify a registered security interest, stolen status or written-off status.
  3. Know what PPSR does not prove. It does not tell you the amount owing, current owner, complete history, odometer accuracy, fines or mechanical condition.
  4. Use an independent qualified mechanic. A roadworthy or safety certificate is not a full pre-purchase inspection and local requirements differ.
  5. Check current registration and seller authority through the state or territory service. Compare name, VIN, registration and transfer documents. Do not copy another jurisdiction's deadline or form.
  6. Price insurance before purchase. Do not assume registration includes cover for damage to your own car or another person's property.
  7. Use a written receipt and cleared payment. Record the parties, date, price, VIN, registration, odometer and included items. Do not release a car or keys because a buyer shows a transfer screenshot.
Consumer protection depends on who sold the car. ACCC says consumer guarantees apply to cars bought from a licensed dealer. A private sale does not provide the same protections, although title, undisturbed possession and no hidden debts or charges still matter. State and territory vehicle rules also apply.

Official sources: PPSR — used vehicle search · Moneysmart — buying and running a car · ACCC — new and second-hand cars · Scamwatch — buying and selling scams | checked 2026-08-30

9. When the plan no longer covers essentials

  1. Stop optional transfers, subscriptions and purchases. Protect food, safe housing, essential medicine, phone access and transport to verified work or support.
  2. Contact the provider before missing a payment. Ask about a hardship team or payment arrangement; do not borrow from an unsolicited message or social-media lender.
  3. Use Moneysmart's financial counselling route for free, confidential help and confirm service scope.
  4. Search Ask Izzy for nearby food, housing, health and other support.
  5. If work is unpaid or deductions are unexplained, preserve rosters, messages, payslips, bank records and your hours, then use the English workplace help route.
  6. If a scam caused the loss, call the bank through an independently verified channel and follow the English recovery sequence.
